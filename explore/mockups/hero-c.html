HØJT DSP

Free plugins for guitar, drums, bass and keys

HØJT is Danish for loud, and for high. HØJT DSP is six plugins, each drawn as a stompbox: an amp with a board of pedals, a drum kit, a pitch shifter, a bass, an ambient pad and glass bells. No sign-up, no ads, no samples to install.

  • Guitar AmpGA-3
  • Drum SynthDS-2
  • Pitch ShifterPS-10
  • Bass SynthBA-1
  • Shimmer PadSP-1
  • Glass BellsGB-1
