Guitar Amp

GA-3 / Effect / mono or stereo in, stereo out

What it is

A whole guitar rig in one window: four amps, each with its own cabinet, and ten pedals around them. Plug a guitar straight into the audio interface and play.

  • Klar is clean and glassy, Brøl a mid-forward crunch, Torden a tight modern high gain, Glød warm and chiming at the edge of breakup
  • Gate, compressor, overdrive, phaser, chorus, flanger, tremolo, cabinet, delay and reverb
  • 0.1 to 0.3 ms of latency, so it can be played live

  1. Download the zip for your system and unpack it.
  2. Copy one of the two plugin files to its folder: .vst3 for most DAWs, or .clap where the DAW supports it.
  3. Let the DAW scan for plugins. They are listed under Hojt Audio.
