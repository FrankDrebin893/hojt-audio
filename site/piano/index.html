Piano viz

See the harmony under your hands while you play

Falling-notes players show which keys to press. The piano viz shows what they mean. Play a MIDI keyboard through any SoundFont or SFZ piano, and read the chord, its Roman numeral, the voicing, the staff and the circle of fifths from the piano stool.

What it shows

  • The chord's name, its Roman numeral with figured bass, the inversion and the voicing, with every note spelled for the key and its interval
  • A grand staff in the key signature, a circle of fifths to pick the key, and a strip of the key's seven chords that lights the one you play and plays any you click
  • Twelve scales: major, three minors, five modes, both pentatonics and the blues. The keys out of the scale are dimmed, and the tonic is marked
  • Knobs for Volume and the metronome's Click, and twelve keys for the key, scale, tempo, recording and songs
  • Six pieces to practise with falling notes, and any .mid file of your own
  • An optional AI assistant to ask about theory (Shift+F2). It needs your own API key for the AI service behind it

What it needs

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • A USB MIDI keyboard or digital piano, or a 5-pin MIDI interface. Without one, the keys on screen can be played with the mouse
  • An ASIO driver for about 5 ms of delay (your audio interface's, or ASIO4ALL), or the WASAPI output built into Windows
  • A piano sound: any .sf2 SoundFont or .sfz piano, for example the free Salamander Grand Piano or one from Musical Artifacts
  • The .exe is not signed yet. If Windows SmartScreen stops it the first time, click More info, then Run anyway

Setup and shortcuts

  1. Unzip and run. Unpack the zip anywhere, your desktop for example, and run PianoMidiVisualizationApp.exe.
  2. Choose a piano sound. Download a .sf2 or .sfz piano and click 2 · Choose a piano sound in the checklist on the display.
  3. Play. Your keyboard and the audio connect by themselves, now and every time after. Choose a key on the toolbar or the circle of fifths, and play.

Your settings, the window, the key and what you taught the keyboard are kept for next time, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PianoMidiVisualizationApp\settings.json.
