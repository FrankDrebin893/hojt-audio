Six plugins / VST3 + CLAP / Windows, macOS, Linux

A guitar amp with a board of thirteen pedals, a drum kit made without samples, a pitch shifter you can play through live, a plucked bass, an ambient pad and glass bells. Each is drawn as a stompbox.

Piano MIDI Visualization / Windows 10 and 11

Plug in a MIDI keyboard and it plays through any SoundFont or SFZ piano while it names the chord you are holding, with its Roman numeral and the notes on a staff. It also plays songs as falling notes and keeps your last ten takes. Keys outside the key you pick are dimmed.

Open source

Both live in one repository on GitHub: the plugins under the GPL, Højte Piano under the MIT licence. If something is broken, or you miss a sound or have an idea, open an issue. Every one is read.

Free to use

Every plugin and Højte Piano are free and open source. If you want to chip in, a coffee on Ko-fi pays for the testing and the time.
