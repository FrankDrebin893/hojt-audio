Free tools for playing music, turned up

HØJT is Danish for loud, and for high. Two things are made under the name: six plugins for your DAW, and an app for practising piano. Both are free, and both are open source.

Six plugins / VST3 + CLAP / Windows, macOS, Linux

A guitar amp with a board of ten pedals, a drum kit made without samples, a low-latency pitch shifter, a plucked bass, an ambient pad and glass bells. Each is drawn as a stompbox, and every one has sound examples on its page.

Piano MIDI Visualization / Windows 10 and 11

Play a MIDI keyboard through any SoundFont or SFZ piano and read the chord, its Roman numeral and the staff while you play. Practise songs with falling notes, record takes, and dim the keys that are out of the key you are in.

Open, all of it

Both live in one repository on GitHub: the plugins under the GPL, the piano viz under the MIT licence. Something broken, a sound you miss, an idea? Every issue is read.
