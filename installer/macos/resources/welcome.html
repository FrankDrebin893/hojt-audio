Højte DSP

Six free plugins: a guitar amp, a drum kit, a pitch shifter, a bass, a pad and bells.

On the next pages you tick which plugins you want, and VST3, CLAP or both. VST3 goes to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and CLAP to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP, for every user of this Mac. Close your DAW first, then let it scan for plugins when you are done. They are listed under Hojt Audio.

Untested. This installer has not been run on a Mac yet, and the plugins have not been tried in a DAW on macOS. If something goes wrong, please say what in an issue on GitHub.

It is not signed, so macOS may refuse to open it: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway next to the message about HojtDSP-macos.pkg.
